EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION
Why FeeSync Lite Exists
The  Indian  private  coaching  market  spans  1.5  million  centers  —  but  the  vast  majority  are  operated  by  a  single
individual who teaches, collects fees, manages parents, and tracks attendance all by themselves. FeeSync Lite is
a  cloud-native,  mobile-first  fee  management  platform  purpose-built  for  these  solo  operators:  home  tutors,  yoga
instructors,  music  teachers,  dance  coaches,  and  single-room  coaching  owners.  It  digitises  every  financial  and
operational touchpoint — from student enrollment and batch assignment through fee collection, WhatsApp receipt
dispatch, attendance tracking, and real-time P&L; visibility — with zero accounting knowledge required.
Value PropositionDescription
Zero Paper100% digital fee records. Searchable by name, phone, or batch instantly.
WhatsApp-FirstInstant receipt and reminder delivery via WhatsApp — the channel every Indian parent uses.
Offline-TolerantFlutter app caches data locally and syncs automatically when connectivity returns.
Full Fee EngineAll 6 fee plan types: monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, annual, custom installment, per-session.
Razorpay NativeUPI QR codes, payment links, online checkout, and auto-reconciliation built in.
AI-Powered9 AI features: overdue predictor, expense OCR, anomaly detection, churn risk, smart summaries.
Zero Accounting SkillsWizard-driven workflows. Any tutor can operate it from day one — no training needed.
Truly Affordablen0 free tier for up to 50 students. n199/month entry price for paid tier.

Database Schema — Core Tables
Architecture: FeeSync Lite uses a single-admin model. Each coaching center has one center_profile record and one
owner_user account. All tables are scoped to owner_id via Row-Level Security — the owner can only ever see their
own data. There are no branch-level scopes, no role hierarchies, and no cross-tenant queries possible.

AUTHENTICATION & ONBOARDING
Login, Session, Setup Flow
FeeSync  Lite  uses  a  single-admin  model.  There  is  exactly  one  account  per  center.  The  owner  is  the  only  user.
There  are  no  staff  invites,  no  permission  tables,  and  no  role-based  access  control.  Authentication  is  handled  by
Supabase Auth. The onboarding flow is designed to reach the first useful action — adding a student and recording
a payment — in under three minutes.

Authentication Specifications
Auth AspectSpecification
Login MethodEmail + password or Google OAuth. Phone OTP login planned for v2.
SessionShort-lived JWT plus refresh token. Auto-expire after 30 days of inactivity.
Two-Factor AuthTOTP-based 2FA — prompted at first login, skippable once. Mandatory for account security.
Password ResetEmail reset link via Supabase Auth. Standard secure flow.
Account DeletionOwner can self-delete the account. All data erased within 30 days per DPDP Act 2023.

Dues Engine
The dues engine auto-generates pending fee records at the start of each billing period. A Supabase Edge Function
runs as a CRON job daily at midnight IST. It re-computes all pending dues, accounts for enrollment date pro-rating,
applies discounts and late fines that accumulate daily after the grace period, handles partial payment tracking, and
processes mid-cycle plan changes. Newly overdue students are flagged automatically and queued for WhatsApp
reminder dispatch.

Online Payment Flow (Razorpay)
•Admin generates a payment link from the student's pending due record
•Link is sent to the parent via WhatsApp, SMS, or email
•Parent opens link and completes payment through Razorpay checkout (UPI, card, or netbanking)
•Razorpay webhook fires to the Vercel Edge Function
•Edge Function verifies the webhook signature and records the payment in Supabase
•PDF receipt is generated automatically and WhatsApp receipt is sent to the parent
•Dashboard KPI cards update in real time via Supabase Realtime subscription
Cheque Lifecycle
Cheques move through a defined lifecycle: Received → Deposited → Cleared or Bounced. On a bounce event, the
payment is automatically reversed, the student's outstanding due is restored, a configurable bounce charge is
added, and an alert is sent to the admin. A clearance reminder is triggered three days after the deposit date if the
status remains Deposited.
Reconciliation Report
The daily and monthly reconciliation view shows total collected by payment mode, Razorpay settlement amounts
versus manually recorded entries, pending cheque clearances, and any discrepancies flagged in red. Exportable
as Excel.

P&L; Statement
The  Profit  &  Loss  statement  is  generated  automatically  from  fee  collection  data  and  expense  entries.  Revenue
equals total fee payments received. Expenses are drawn from all expense entries in the selected period. Net Profit
equals  Revenue  minus  Expenses.  The  statement  is  displayed  as  a  monthly  bar  chart  and  a  detailed  table.  Both
accrual basis and cash basis views are available. Annual view is supported. Exportable as a PDF formatted for a
CA or accountant.
